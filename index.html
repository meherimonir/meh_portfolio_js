01 — About

Machine learning for decisions that matter.

I'm a final-year Computer Science & Engineering student at International Islamic University Chittagong (IIUC) and a Research Intern at EliteLab (ELITE Research Lab LLC), where I help design experiments, review literature and analyse data on ongoing projects.

So far that has meant forecasting dengue from search trends, explaining food-price predictions with SHAP, and — for my thesis — spatiotemporal GNNs that predict disease risk from the environment. Increasingly, I care about interpretability, uncertainty estimation and trustworthy AI for high-stakes domains — and I'm open to new directions in graph-based learning and human-centred AI.

Research interests

  • Graph-based learning

    Spatiotemporal GNNs and graph attention networks.

  • Healthcare AI

    Disease-risk prediction and public-health forecasting.

  • Trustworthy & interpretable ML

    Interpretability, uncertainty estimation and reliable decision-making.
